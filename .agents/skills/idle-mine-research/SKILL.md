# Project research workflow

1. Read `docs/knowledge/research-workflow.md`, `parity-contract.md`, and the relevant focused page.
2. Apply the evidence hierarchy: final Remix source, runtime/archive behavior, original Remix assets/UI, developer statements/wiki, original Idle Mine lineage, Remux prior art, community reports.
3. Prefer official first-party sources. Pin repository revisions and record source URLs, dates, license evidence, and exact paths. Use `.research/` only for disposable local data.
4. Classify every conclusion as verified behavior, project decision, hypothesis, known defect, or post-parity proposal. Preserve conflicting evidence instead of silently choosing.
5. Put durable knowledge in the canonical responsibility-specific doc, add fixtures where behavior is testable, and update the manifest/matrix/status as appropriate.
6. Do not port upstream code/assets without a provenance and notice review in `docs/knowledge/ip-provenance.md`.
