# Post-parity backlog

Everything here is a proposal, not authorization to implement it before parity-v1. Ideas may be reprioritized after the compatibility baseline exists.

See [criticisms and reports](criticisms-and-reports.md) for the research context. Items below are proposals only.

## Player experience

- Revisit gem grind and long dead zones while preserving the original game as a selectable compatibility experience if needed.
- Crafting history, clearer probability statistics, quality-of-life for bulk craft, and carefully evaluated pity mechanics.
- Internal auto-repeat or click-rate normalization to reduce dependence on external autoclickers.
- Revisit the active versus idle incentive loop and one-hit farming choices without assuming that the newest object should always be optimal.
- Revisit story pacing and objective rewards while retaining the complete Remix story as the compatibility mode.
- Evaluate whether prestige or a new post-Universe system belongs in Beyond; prestige is not assumed or required by genre convention.
- Accessibility and localization.
- A mobile-specific layout that retains systems, content, hierarchy, and identity.

## Reliability and platforms

- Cloud saves, stronger recovery UI, export/import improvements, Steam integration, achievements, and platform adapters.
- Improved background simulation and offline calculations after documenting legacy outcomes.
- Modding and creator tools.

## Content

- New story, content beyond existing procedural boundaries, post-Universe progression, challenges, new resources, and balance changes.
- Richer visual effects while preserving the original presentation as a reference or mode.

## Gate

Do not pull a proposal into implementation before parity-v1 unless a complete entry is approved in [behavioral exceptions](BEHAVIORAL_EXCEPTIONS.md).
