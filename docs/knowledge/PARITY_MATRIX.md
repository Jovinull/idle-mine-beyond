**Settings dark Export alternate viewport (2026-11-08):** after changing preferences, saving, and exporting, the live
pinned-Remix/Beyond Settings comparison now also matches the full 1366x768 screen in dark theme. The focused visual
spec passed 3/3 in Chromium; other Settings state and viewport combinations remain open.

**Money aligned-purchase visual (2026-11-08):** the pinned Control purchase
from Active Power level 99 reaches level 100 with one purchase and spends the
exact Money balance. The resulting full-screen Money view matches Remix in
light and dark at 1440x900; other purchase states and balances remain open.

Status reflects evidence and validation, not code presence. Unstarted areas remain **not started**. “Foundation only” means the test harness exists but no game behavior has been implemented or certified. Every row is mapped to source functions, relevant branches, fixtures, tests, and evidence limits in the [source-to-test traceability audit](parity-traceability/README.md). A row may be proposed as Certified only after every relevant path in its map is covered and all evidence columns are satisfied. Non-exhaustive formula/RNG inputs do not block certification when their source boundaries, fixed-seed sample, and no-divergence differential meet the map rule; unresolved branches, save/platform domains, and visual state gaps remain open. The parity-v1 gate covers the web build; native packaging, native storage, and running Tauri WebView behavior are outside that gate.

and 42490501482 both reproduce exactly two differing pixels at `(15,632)` and

two Chapter 3 light pixels and two Chapter 4 dark pixels remain at 1366x768;

captured computed CSS properties, objective text geometry, and regular/bold
Work Sans checks now match across source and Beyond, while the raster pixels
still differ. CI run 42500838165 confirms exact objective text/markup and
Chromium reports `Work Sans` / `WorkSans-Regular` for the bold node in both
pages, with 16 glyphs each. CI run 42503025927 shows the source article is

and `article.story`; matched ancestor nodes have equal computed CSS and

browser tests; only Chapter 3 light failed and Chapter 4 dark passed. The strict
comparison remains unchanged, without tolerance or mask; later Linux viewports
remain unverified.
